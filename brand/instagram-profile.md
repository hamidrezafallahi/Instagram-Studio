## Links (up to 5 in the bio)

| Link | URL pattern |
|---|---|
| 1. Website | `https://hamidrezafalahi.ir/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bio` **[confirm domain]** |
| 2. Portfolio / case studies | `…/portfolio?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bio-portfolio` |
| 3. Contact or consultation form | `…/contact?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bio-contact` |

Story link stickers use `utm_campaign=story-<content-id>`; never put personal data in URLs.
