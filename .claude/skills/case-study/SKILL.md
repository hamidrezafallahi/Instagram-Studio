# Case study

Case studies are 30% of the mix and the strongest lead driver, because they are proof. That only works if every word is true and every client agreed.

## Gate 0: intake (blocking)

Read `projects/<slug>/project.md`. Continue only if:

- **Consent level A or B** is recorded with evidence (`projects/README.md`). Level C, or no record, means stop and tell Hamidreza what's missing.
- Every metric has a **before value, an after value, a date and a source** (analytics export, screenshot, client report). Anything unsourced is left out or kept as a qualitative statement.
- Screenshots in `assets/screenshots/<slug>/` are **redacted**: names, phones, emails, addresses, order IDs, confidential prices.
- Testimonial text is **verbatim** and approved by the client.

If something is missing, write the questions into the project file's "Open questions" and stop. Don't fill gaps with plausible numbers.

## Narrative (every format)

Context (who, what business; anonymised for consent B) → problem (in the client's words) → constraints (time, budget, legacy) → approach (2–3 key decisions and *why*) → build highlights (real screenshots) → result (numbers with date and source) → quote → CTA.

## Package

1. **Carousel** (8–10 slides) with the `carousel` skill: Ink theme, figures with captions and mono source lines, one big-metric slide per real metric (two at most).
2. **Reel walkthrough** (45–60 s) with `reel-script`: a screen recording of the finished product, voiceover on decisions and results.
3. **Stories** with `story-sequence`: a teaser (results first) and a before/after.
4. **Website outline** for a case-study page (headings and copy blocks), if Hamidreza has a site.
5. Add the content IDs to the project file's "Content derivatives".

## Extra review steps

- If the consent record says the client reviews content, add a **client review** before G4 and record the client's OK in the project file.
- A collaborator post (shared with the client's account) needs the client's explicit agreement and their public Business or Creator handle.
