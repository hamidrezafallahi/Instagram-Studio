# Reel script

Reels are the discovery engine: most reach to non-followers comes from them. Hamidreza films; Claude writes the plan. No AI video, no AI voice, no stock footage.

## Read first

- The slot in the month plan; `brand/voice-and-tone.md` §3 (spoken register); `brand/visual-identity.md` §4 (safe zones); `strategy/cta-library.md`
- Template: `content/templates/reel.md`

## Procedure

1. **Brief:** persona, problem, takeaway, proof, CTA, target length. Education: 20–35 s. Case-study walkthrough: 45–60 s.
2. **Hook (0–2 s).** A visual and a line that land together: a real page loading slowly, a messy spreadsheet, a before/after split, plus a line that names the viewer's problem or promises a test. Write 3 options and recommend one.
3. **Beats.** Problem (2–8 s) → 2–3 value beats (one sentence of voiceover and one visual each) → proof or demo → CTA (last 3–5 s). Cut anything that doesn't move the viewer toward the takeaway.
4. **Voiceover** in light spoken Persian, the way Hamidreza actually talks («بیاید یک تست ساده بکنیم»). Short sentences. At about 2.5 words per second, a 30-second Reel holds roughly 70–80 words.
5. **On-screen text:** at most 7 words per card, standard written Persian, inside the safe zone (y 250–1580). It has to carry the message with the sound off, because many people watch muted.
6. **Shot list** per beat: A-roll (to camera) or B-roll (screen recording, close-up of code or a device), noting any client data to blur.
7. **Cover** from `MASTER · Reel cover`: a title of 6 words or fewer, inside y 300–1600.
8. **Caption** with `persian-caption`. It should add a point the video didn't make, not repeat it.
9. **Filming checklist** for Hamidreza from the template (vertical 1080 × 1920, light, audio, notifications off).
10. **QA** with `brand-voice-qa`, save to `content/01-drafts/<ID>_reel_<slug>.md`, and ask for G2.

## Example: hook options for IG-002 (five-second test)

1. «این تست ۵ ثانیه‌ای نشان می‌دهد سایت‌تان مشتری را گیج می‌کند یا نه.» Recommended: a promise plus curiosity.
2. «سایت شما در ۵ ثانیهٔ اول چه می‌گوید؟»
3. «اگر بازدیدکننده به این سؤال جواب ندهد، سایت‌تان مشکل دارد.»
