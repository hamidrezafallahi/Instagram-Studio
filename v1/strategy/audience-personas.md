# Audience personas

Four buyer personas from the brief. They are hypotheses until confirmed by real DMs and calls: update "in their words" with real (anonymised) quotes from `inbox/triage/` and `leads/`.

---

## P1. E-commerce owner («مدیر فروشگاه اینترنتی»)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Owner or co-founder of a shop that sells mostly through Instagram and DMs, or runs an aging store site. 1–10 people. Decides alone or with a partner. |
| **Situation** | Orders arrive in DMs, payment by card-to-card transfer, stock tracked by hand. Or a slow store built years ago, which nobody maintains. |
| **Triggers** | DM volume is unmanageable; a seasonal peak is coming (Yalda, Nowruz); fear of depending on one platform; a competitor launched a store. |
| **Desired outcome** | Orders and payments that run without them, a faster store, fewer lost customers, independence from DMs. |

**In their words** (hypothesis): «دایرکت‌ها آن‌قدر زیاد شده که سفارش‌ها گم می‌شود.» · «اینستاگرام که هست، سایت به چه دردی می‌خورد؟» · «یک بار سایت ساختم و طراحش غیبش زد.» · «گرفتن درگاه پرداخت دردسر دارد.»

| Objection | How content answers it |
|---|---|
| "Instagram is enough" | Platform-dependence risk, order management, findability in Google search |
| Cost | Phased build (launch core, add later), cost drivers explained honestly |
| Past developer vanished | Process transparency, milestones, the client owns domain, hosting and code |
| Too technical | Plain-language explanations, what the client must prepare |

**Proof they need:** order flow demo, admin panel walkthrough, speed before/after, eNamad and payment gateway readiness, a real store case study.
**Content angles:** DM-to-store migration, checkout leaks, product page anatomy, speed and mobile, Nowruz readiness timeline.
**Qualifying signals:** existing sales volume, a product catalog, a timeline before a seasonal peak, budget awareness.
**Not a fit:** wants a copy of a large marketplace for a tiny budget; expects a store "by tomorrow".

## P2. Education institute or academy («مدیر آموزشگاه / آکادمی»)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Director or owner of a language institute, art or music school, tutoring or test-prep centre, or an independent course creator. |
| **Situation** | Registration by phone and WhatsApp, payments checked manually, schedules shared as images, no online course platform or a costly third-party one. |
| **Triggers** | New term enrollment (Shahrivar–Mehr, Dey–Bahman, summer); launching online courses; staff overloaded at registration time. |
| **Desired outcome** | Online registration and payment, a clear course catalog, a student panel, less admin work every term. |

**In their words** (hypothesis): «ثبت‌نام‌ها را دستی در اکسل وارد می‌کنیم.» · «هر ترم کلی پیام می‌آید که کلاس کی شروع می‌شود.» · «پلتفرم‌های آماده کمیسیون زیادی برمی‌دارند.»

| Objection | How content answers it |
|---|---|
| "We are not a tech company" | Show the registration flow from a student's phone; simple admin |
| Cost vs. ready-made platforms | Honest comparison: when a ready platform is enough, when custom pays off |
| Timing | Plan backwards from the term start date |

**Proof they need:** registration flow screenshots, admin time saved (real numbers), student panel demo.
**Content angles:** enrollment funnel, course page anatomy, ready LMS vs. custom, term-start checklist.
**Qualifying signals:** recurring terms, student volume, a launch date.
**Not a fit:** wants a full LMS clone with no defined courses yet.

## P3. Companies and exhibition businesses («شرکت‌ها و کسب‌وکارهای نمایشگاهی»)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Marketing or sales manager or owner at a manufacturer, B2B supplier or importer; exhibitors at trade fairs; event organisers. |
| **Situation** | An outdated corporate site, PDF catalogs, Persian-only pages despite foreign buyers, no lead capture at exhibitions. |
| **Triggers** | An upcoming exhibition, export plans, a rebrand, a new product line, an embarrassing site in front of a key client. |
| **Desired outcome** | A credible, fast corporate site, a browsable catalog, inquiry forms that reach sales, multilingual pages, exhibition leads captured. |

**In their words** (hypothesis): «سایت‌مان مال ده سال پیش است و خجالت می‌کشیم لینکش را بدهیم.» · «برای نمایشگاه کاتالوگ PDF می‌فرستیم و کسی نگاهش نمی‌کند.» · «مشتری خارجی داریم ولی سایت فقط فارسی است.»

| Objection | How content answers it |
|---|---|
| Approval chains, slow decisions | Clear scope documents and phased delivery that are easy to approve internally |
| "A site doesn't sell B2B" | Inquiry flows, catalog search, lead capture at fairs, real examples |
| Deadline (fair date) | A realistic timeline back-planned from the fair date |

**Proof they need:** redesign before/after, catalog and inquiry flow, multilingual structure, exhibition landing page with QR.
**Content angles:** credibility signals, catalog systems, multilingual architecture, QR from the booth to a landing page.
**Qualifying signals:** a fair date, export intent, a clear decision-maker.
**Not a fit:** "just a nice-looking site" with no owner for content.

## P4. Businesses that need a custom web application («کسب‌وکار نیازمند وب‌اپلیکیشن اختصاصی»)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Founder, operations manager or technical lead at a growing company or startup. |
| **Situation** | Operations run on spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups; off-the-shelf software doesn't match the process; manual reports. |
| **Triggers** | Process breaks at scale, errors and duplicated work, an investor or client demo, a new service line. |
| **Desired outcome** | One system for the core process, reliable data, dashboards, integrations, a first usable version soon. |

**In their words** (hypothesis): «کل عملیات‌مان روی چند فایل اکسل و گروه واتس‌اپ است.» · «نرم‌افزارهای آماده با فرایند ما جور نیست.» · «می‌ترسیم پروژه نصفه بماند.»

| Objection | How content answers it |
|---|---|
| Cost and risk | MVP first; scope by impact; fixed milestones |
| Abandoned-project fear | Documentation, code ownership, handover, transparent progress |
| Build vs. buy | An honest decision rule, including "buy" when it's enough |

**Proof they need:** redacted dashboards, architecture explained simply, process artifacts, code quality habits.
**Content angles:** "Excel → dashboard", MVP scoping, build vs. buy, integrations explained.
**Qualifying signals:** a defined process owner, a measurable pain, budget for phased work.
**Not a fit:** a vague "app like [famous app]" with no defined users or process.
